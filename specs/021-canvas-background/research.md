# Research: Canvas Background

- **Decision**: replace `Presentation.createForWidthAndHeight(..., SCALE_TO_FIT)` with `FitWithBackgroundEffect(width, height, canvas)` only when the canvas is Colour or Blur. The effect declares the output size as the project frame; the shader computes the fitted rectangle of the input inside the output (same maths as the plain fit) and, for pixels outside it, returns the colour (premultiplied alpha 1) or a blurred sample of the input stretched to cover the frame. **Why**: one pass, no compositor, nothing else in the chain changes; Black keeps the proven path so existing exports stay identical.
- **Blur**: a 13-tap kernel on a cover-scaled, low-detail sample (offsets scaled by the strength: radius 2%, 4%, 8% of the frame height) with a darkening of 15% so the foreground stays readable. The blurred copy is taken from the clip's picture at the point of the fit, so a filter, effect, or transform applied before the fit shows in the blur; the filter and effect after the fit apply to the whole frame including the background, so a look is uniform.
- **Order caveat**: in the existing chain the fit comes before the filter, effect, and framing, so those act on the frame with its background; the framing zoom (100% or more) scales the background with the picture, which is acceptable and documented.
- **Rejected**: a second video input for the background (forces the multi-input path for every project); drawing the background in Compose (the export would differ).
- **Persistence**: Room 17 to 18 auto migration with defaults `Black`, `-16777216`, `Medium`.
- **Editor**: the Effects tab bar with no clip selected has Background; the panel is a sheet with Black, a Colour row of 12 swatches and a custom button (the existing colour picker), and a Blur row with Light, Medium, Strong; the choice is applied at once and each tap is one undo step.
