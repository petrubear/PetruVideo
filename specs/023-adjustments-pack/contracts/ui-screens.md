# UI: Adjustments Pack

Filters, Adjust: a segmented control "Light | Tone | Colour | Detail". Light: Brightness, Contrast, Exposure. Tone: Gamma, Highlights, Shadows. Colour: Saturation, Warmth, Tint. Detail: Hue, Sharpen, Vignette. A Reset action below puts everything at neutral. All sliders labelled, touch targets at least 48 dp.
