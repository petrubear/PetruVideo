# UI Contract: Keyframes for Video Clips

- **Tool**: selecting a video clip shows the existing "Keyframes" clip tool (phone: sheet; unfolded: inspector section; tabletop: sheet in the lower half).
- **Panel**: unchanged controls (Add, Previous, Next, Copy here, Delete, Linear/Ease) plus Zoom and Opacity sliders; Pan is by dragging the preview.
- **Diamonds**: 48 dp targets in a strip along the bottom of the selected clip in the video lane, clear of the trim handles.
- **Preview**: with a video clip selected, pinch zooms, one-finger drag pans; both auto-key at the playhead.
- **Messages**: "Too many keyframes (50 max)" when the limit is hit.
- **Accessibility**: the same labels as overlays, plus "Zoom" and "Pan" descriptions; contrast and 48 dp rules apply.
